## Saros: astronomy and calendar are different scales

[NASA, Eclipses and the Saros](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/SEsaros/SEsaros.html) defines the astronomical recurrence as approximately **6,585.3 days: 18 years, 11 days and 8 hours**. After a Saros, eclipses have similar geometry; the extra fraction of a day means the next solar eclipse is visible from a different part of Earth. Do not depict it as an exact whole-year interval or an eclipse returning to the same location every 18 years.

The [original Rolex 2012 launch press kit](https://www.thewatch.co.il/image/users/190146/ftp/my_files/ROLEX/Sky-Dweller_EN.pdf), PDF page 6, directly connects the name and planetary/satellite architecture to that astronomical inspiration. The watch's mechanism instead handles the **civil calendar**: a date-driven satellite orbits a fixed wheel once per month; four fingers correspond to April, June, September and November. At those month ends, an extra impulse produces the rapid double date advance from 30 through 31 to 1. February still requires correction on 1 March. The watch does not calculate eclipses or run its annual calendar on an eighteen-year cycle.

For visual continuity, let the Sun/Earth/Moon composition dissolve into fixed wheel/satellite/fingers. Make that transition a metaphor. The astronomical orbit is not a kinematic or dimensional model of the watch. The main technical chapter must explain the extra impulse and the February exception after this invitation.

## Accuracy boundaries

- Rolex dates the GMT-Master to **1955**. Its more detailed [2024 GMT-Master II brochure](https://newsroom-content.rolex.com/-/media/project/rolex/newsroom/rolex/rolex-newsroom-int/2024-04/gmt-master-ii/documents/brochures/03_gmt-master-ii_brochure_english.pdf), timeline section, dates becoming Pan Am's official watch to **1959**. Avoid compressing the partnership into a 1955 commissioning story.
- The 1931 claim concerns Rolex's **Perpetual rotor**, not the invention of every form of automatic winding.
- Present the 1926, 1931 and 1955 moments through abstract sealed-case, rotor and dual-time motifs unless their actual historical watches are separately researched. Do not label a modern Sky-Dweller silhouette as one of those historical models.
- The final 2012 reveal must retain the selected Roman dial and calibre 9001. Current Rolex product pages describe a later generation; use the original launch PDF for this chapter's movement details.
